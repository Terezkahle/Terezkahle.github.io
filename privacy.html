Privacy policy

Sekretarka is a personal application used only by its developer on her own computer.

  • It accesses Google Docs and Google Drive only in the developer's own Google account, to read and edit her own documents.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is stored only locally on the developer's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sekretarka's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
